## Press pitch (English)

**Subject:** Data: Open doctor jobs across Europe rose 11% in November 2026.

Workog counts every open job for doctors in Germany and across Europe once a day. Our November 2026 numbers may be useful for your coverage of hospital staffing:

- At the end of Nov 20, 2026, Workog listed 26,222 open jobs for doctors in 25 countries, up 11% from 23,730 on Nov 4, 2026.
- General practice had the most open jobs (4,170), followed by Psychiatry (2,070) and Internal medicine (1,910).
- Germany had 5,926 of them (23%); North Rhine-Westphalia had the most of any German state (933).
- General practice grew the most: +471 open jobs.

The full tables (by specialty, career level, country, German state and hospital) are at https://workog.com/reports/medicine/2026-11. The data is free to use with a link; we're happy to send a breakdown for a specific specialty or region.

_Method:_ Workog fetches jobs for doctors from public job services (Bundesagentur für Arbeit, EURES, NHS Jobs, Arbetsförmedlingen, job-room.ch) and hospital groups' own careers sites once a day, and counts the jobs open at the end of the UTC day. Specialty and career level are read from the job title; jobs that name neither are counted in the total only. Changes compare the month's last day with the last day before the month began.

_Citation:_ Workog, “Doctor job market: November 2026”, https://workog.com/reports/medicine/2026-11, data as of Nov 20, 2026.
